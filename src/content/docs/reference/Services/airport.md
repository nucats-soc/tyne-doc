Airport is the codename for the NUCATS website, located at https://nucats.org

This site is built with Svelte and Tailwind, with multiple third party connections added in.

The site connects to the NUCATS Notion organisation to fetch data about committee members and events, and uses Google Maps to provide information on event locations.

If you have any other questions about the site, please direct them to the Tech Team in Discord.

https://github.com/nucats-soc/airport
