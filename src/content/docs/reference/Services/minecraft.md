We have a Minecraft server for members to play on!

You can play by connecting to `mc.nucats.org`.

## How do I get on the whitelist?
We have a whitelist in place to prevent random people from playing on this server.

If you would like to be on this whitelist, you will need to create a ticket in the NUCATS Discord server, and request to be added, make sure to send your IGN before we ask to make things quicker.

## Rules
In summary, don't be an idiot.
- No griefing other people's builds.
  - This doesn't include placing down a utility block temporarily, you're fine to do that.
- No spamming in chat.
- Client mods are allowed to an extent.
  - You can use stuff like minimaps, improvedchat, utility stuff basically.
  - We're not 2b2t, so don't be joining with anything stupid loaded though.
  - If you're going to use something like Baritone, at least don't make it obvious and give yourself a stupid advantage, we have automated alerts for stuff like that.
- PVP is allowed, as long as it doesn't turn into bullying.

## I was banned?
You were probably banned for a reason, we don't unban people unless there is a very good reason to.

If you want to try your luck at being unbanned, make a ticket in the Discord server, or email soc.computingtechnology@ncl.ac.uk, we will see what you were originally banned for, and hear out your reason for being unbanned.
