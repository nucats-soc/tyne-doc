---
title: Naming Scheme
description: Naming Scheme all NUCATS projects and services should adhere to
---
:::note
All NUCATS projects, infrastructure, etc... follow a naming convention, if you're smart, you might've already figured it out, but it's also below so people know.
:::

- You should name after a station on the Tyne and Wear Metro
  - Keep the name the same, or make a small alteration, for example:
    - Discord Bot is called `manor`, named after Manors
    - Website is called `airport`, named after Newcastle Airport
    - This site is called `tyne-doc`, named after Tyne Dock
  - You should **not** use any station past Pelaw on the Green Line
    - Every station except those in Sunderland are fair game
    - After the Washington loop opens, you should consider any station past South Hylton, back towards Newcastle.
  - In the (unlikely) event that we run out of stations, consider the following current/historic rail lines with stops in the North East
    - Tyne Valley Line
    - East Coast Main Line
    - Northumberland Line
    - Durham Coast Line
    - Leamside Line
